## Read the verdict off the board

King-and-pawn endings obey three counting rules. Learn them cold and you'll know the result before either side moves.

- **King two ranks in front of its pawn: won. Always.** Whoever moves. With that much lead you can always regain the opposition when it matters.
- **King one rank in front: won only with the opposition** — or a spare pawn move to steal it. Without either, correct defence holds.
- **King on the 6th rank, in front of the pawn: won, full stop.** Past every defence, no opposition needed.

## The exception that owns two files

None of this applies to rook pawns. On the a- and h-files the defender only needs the corner, and Tier 1's fortress rules take over. Check the file before you trust the verdict.
