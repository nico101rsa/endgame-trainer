## Which pairs actually mate

Down to king and two minor pieces against a bare king? The verdict depends entirely on which two:

- **Two bishops: win.** Side by side they build a diagonal wall; your king herds the enemy king to any corner and the wall closes.
- **Bishop + knight: win, but hard.** The mate only works in a corner of the *bishop's* colour and can take thirty-plus moves. At this level, know the verdict — you don't need to execute it yet.
- **Two knights: draw.** The mating net can't be built without stalemating first. It cannot be forced.

## Why this matters before you can do it

These verdicts decide your trades. Simplifying into two bishops is cashing in; into bishop + knight is signing up for homework; into two knights is handing over half a point. And when the win is there, one habit rules the endgame: **check for stalemate before every quiet move** — a cornered king with no moves is either mated or your draw thrown away.
