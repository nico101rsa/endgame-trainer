## A whole extra piece, and still a draw

White has a bishop and a pawn more — and no win. It happens on exactly two files. A rook pawn queens in the corner, and if the bishop doesn't control the corner's colour, it can never evict a king that camps there. Stalemate guards everything.

**The instant check:** look at the promotion corner's colour. Bishop matches it — normal win. Bishop doesn't — *wrong bishop*, and the defender draws if the king reaches the corner.

## Both sides' plans in one line each

- **Defending:** get your king to the corner by the shortest road, and stay. Nothing else matters. The road is often a straight diagonal — and note the punchline: a diagonal of one colour can never be blocked by a bishop of the other.
- **Attacking:** the only winning idea is to fence the defending king out of the corner before it arrives. Once it's in, put the pieces away.
