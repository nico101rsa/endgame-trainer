## The pawn's prize squares

Every pawn has **key squares**: stand your king on one, and the win is yours *no matter whose move it is*. All the opposition tricks of Tier 1 are just tools for reaching them.

- **Pawn on its 2nd, 3rd or 4th rank:** the three squares **two ranks ahead**. For a pawn on e3, that's d5, e5, f5.
- **Pawn on its 5th or 6th rank:** the zone grows — the three squares **directly in front**. For a pawn on e5: d6, e6, f6.

## Attack and defence in one rule

Attacking, aim your king at the key squares, not the pawn at the queening square. If the defender watches two of them, race to the third.

Defending, the whole job is to make every key square unreachable — cover them, or stand on one. That is why the post directly in front of the pawn is so strong: one king covers the whole row.
