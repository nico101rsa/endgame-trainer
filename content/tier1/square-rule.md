## Can the king catch it?

A passed pawn races for the back rank; the defending king gives chase. You don't need to count moves — draw a box.

From the pawn, walk the diagonal to the promotion rank; that diagonal and the promotion rank frame **the square of the pawn**. If the chasing king, on its move, can step inside the square, it catches the pawn. If it can't, the pawn queens.

- Pawn still on its starting square? Its first step can be two — draw the box from one rank further on.
- If it's the pawn's turn, it moves first: check the box you'll face **after** the push.
- The border counts as inside.

One glance replaces eight moves of counting. And it cuts both ways: when the box says your own pawn wins the race, push it — it doesn't need an escort.
