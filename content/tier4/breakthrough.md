## Three pawns crash through

*Scaffold lesson — the full Tier 4 set (rook activity, R+P vs R cut-offs, minor pieces vs pawns, opposite-coloured bishops) arrives with a later milestone.*

Three pawns abreast on the fifth against three at home can force a passed pawn **with no king in sight**: the middle pawn gives itself up, twice over, and one outside pawn walks through the wreckage.

The order is everything: **middle pawn first.** Capture toward the a-side and the c-pawn advances; capture toward the c-side and the a-pawn does. Either way the survivor is past every defender.

Count before you leap — you're spending two pawns to promote one, so the new queen must arrive *first*, in time to hunt down whatever your opponent got running in return. And when the trio faces *you*: the same push, one move earlier, kills the whole trick forever.
