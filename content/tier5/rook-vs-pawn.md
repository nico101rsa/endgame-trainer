## Rook against the running pawn

*Scaffold lesson — the full Tier 5 set (short-side defence, Vancura, bishop vs knight, queen endings, corresponding squares) arrives with a later milestone.*

A rook against a king escorting a passed pawn is a race with two outcomes and one deciding factor: **where is your king?**

- **King close:** you win. Drive the enemy king in front of its own pawn with side-checks, post your own king on the knight-file, and the promotion walks into a back-rank mate — the new queen dies behind its own king.
- **King far:** the rook fights alone, and alone it can only harass — the king can shield the pawn or advance it, never both, but the moment your rook commits, it's rook-for-pawn and a draw.

Know which race you're in before move one; the techniques don't mix.
