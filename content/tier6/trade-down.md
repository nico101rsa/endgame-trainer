## The most committal move in chess

*Scaffold lesson — the full Tier 6 set (R+2 vs R+1, connected passers vs rook, same-colour bishop endings, fortress survey) arrives with a later milestone.*

Trading the last pieces into a pure pawn ending is irreversible: no perpetuals, no fortresses, no swindles. Just opposition, key squares and the square rule — which means the verdict is **computable before you trade**, with tools you already own from Tiers 1 and 2.

That cuts both ways. Rook endings are famously drawish — a defending king planted in front of the pawn holds Philidor forever. The *same position minus the rooks* can be a trivially won king-and-pawn ending. So the winning skill is arithmetic, not technique: read the pawn ending to the end, then trade only when the verdict is already yours.
