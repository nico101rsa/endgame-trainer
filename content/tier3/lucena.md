## The position every rook ending flows toward

*Scaffold lesson — the full Tier 3 set (triangulation, passed pawns, Philidor, queen vs 7th-rank pawn) arrives with a later milestone.*

Rook + pawn vs rook, pawn one step from queening, your king stuck in front of it, checks raining in. This is the **Lucena position**, and it's won — by *building a bridge*.

The idea in one line: lift your rook to the **fourth rank**, walk the king out through the checks, and when the checks run out of room, interpose the rook. It shields the king, the checks end, the pawn queens.

The defender's mirror is the **Philidor position** — rook on the third rank until the pawn crosses, then checks from behind forever. The demo shows the attacking half; drill both sides in the full lesson later.
