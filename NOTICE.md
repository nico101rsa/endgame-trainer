solution trees) is © 2026 Nico McDonald, licensed

Every line of it is written from scratch. The underlying endgame theory —
opposition, key squares, the square rule, Lucena, Philidor, Vancura, the
wrong-colour bishop draw — is several centuries of public chess knowledge and
belongs to nobody. What is authored here is the explanation, the choice of
positions, and the hand-built solution trees.

## On Silman

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from Jeremy
Silman's *Complete Endgame Course*. Structuring an endgame curriculum by
playing strength is an idea, and ideas are not owned; the book's text,
diagrams and layout are, and none of them appear here. No passage is quoted,
paraphrased or transcribed, and no position is copied from it. Where a topic
appears in both — as it does in every endgame book written since Lucena in
1497 — it is because the theory is shared, not the writing.

See the "Original content" rule in
[docs/ENDGAME_TRAINER_SPEC.md](docs/ENDGAME_TRAINER_SPEC.md) §2, which binds
every lesson authored in this repo.
